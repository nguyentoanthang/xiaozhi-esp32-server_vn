# Open Letter to Developers

"The duck is the first to feel the warming spring river, just as the pufferfish season begins!"

Dear friend, I am John, a Java programmer at an ordinary company. Today, with the utmost sincerity, I am writing this open letter to you, who love AI technology and innovation.

Half a year ago I saw many excellent projects, such as `Dify` and `Chat2DB` and other AI-related projects, and I thought how wonderful it would be to take part in them. Sadly, "there is no way to serve the country, and I have just been writing code for ten years in vain."

In early 2025 I came across a video by Brother Xia's team. I was very curious how he did it, and I wanted to replicate their backend service and build a low-cost, civilian-grade Jarvis. Unfortunately, what I have built so far is still just an "artificial idiot": low concurrency, no soul, slow responses, and plenty of bugs.

Brother Xia's team is who we learn from, and I would love to have a Xiaozhi backend service as smart as theirs. But I can also understand Brother Xia's decision not to open-source it. "A single blooming flower does not make spring; when a hundred flowers bloom, spring fills the garden." The era when AI blossoms everywhere may well be realized in our generation, and we can use our own hands to build a low-cost, civilian-grade Jarvis. I personally believe that whatever he can do, we can do too; it is only a matter of time. I call it "our pilgrimage for scriptures".

So what difficulties will we meet on this pilgrimage? I think no fewer than eighty-one trials. Along the way, all kinds of demons will inevitably appear; of course there will also be immortals secretly helping us, and others joining the pilgrimage party.

If you find the above funny, then I feel very lucky. If I can make you smile for five seconds out of your 30,000-odd days of life, that counts as a contribution to you.

Will the idea of a low-cost, civilian-grade Jarvis fail? I don't know, but in the lives of ordinary people like us, isn't that kind of failure quite common?

One thing is certain about the future: someone will fully replicate the features of Brother Xia's team and build a low-cost, civilian-grade Jarvis. Will this project be us?

I look forward to moving forward with you and creating the future together.

John, 2025.3.11, Guangzhou

# Appendix: Development Contribution Guide
## Project Goals

1. **A low-cost, civilian-grade Jarvis solution**  

2. **A solution for smart interaction with peripheral hardware**  

## Join Us

We warmly welcome like-minded friends to join us and contribute to the project. You can see the features we plan to implement soon at [this link](https://github.com/users/xinnan-tech/projects/3); items in the feature list that have not yet been assigned to anyone are exactly where we urgently need your help. Ways to participate:

### 1. Become a regular contributor

Fork the project and submit a PR; after review by the developers, it is merged into the main branch.

### 2. Become a developer

After you have submitted 3 valid PRs in total, you can contact the group owner to apply to become a developer. The group owner will invite you to a separate developer group to discuss the future of the project together.

## Developer Workflow

1. **Create a new branch**  
   Develop each feature on a new branch. The branch name should be concise and clear so that the feature it implements is obvious at a glance, to avoid duplicated work.

2. **Submit a PR for review**  
   After the feature is complete, submit a PR on GitHub. Other developers will review it, and once approved it is merged into the main branch.
